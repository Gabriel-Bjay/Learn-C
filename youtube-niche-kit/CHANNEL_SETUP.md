# Work Rewired — channel setup

## 1. Must-do before your first upload: verify the channel
YouTube Studio → **Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility → Intermediate features → Verify phone number**.

Without this you **can't upload videos longer than 15 minutes** and **can't add custom thumbnails**. Our videos are 13–20 minutes and thumbnails matter a lot, so do this first. It takes about 2 minutes by SMS.

## 2. Channel description
Studio → **Customization → Profile → Description**. Paste:

```
The future of work, decoded — with real numbers.

Work Rewired breaks down the money and technology behind how we work: how companies really make (and lose) money, who gets paid along the way, and how tech and AI are changing businesses and jobs.

Every video picks one question — like "How does Uber make money on a single ride, and what does the driver keep?" — and answers it with sourced data, clear math and no hype.

You'll find:
• The economics of owning a business — from AI data centers to software companies
• How companies make money on a single order, ride or subscription
• Why big companies collapse, and what it meant for the people who worked there
• What tech and AI are really doing to jobs, pay and careers

New breakdown every week. Sources are listed in every video description.

Not financial advice — just the numbers, explained.
```

If you can't keep a weekly schedule yet, change "New breakdown every week" to "New breakdowns regularly."

## 3. Banner text
Your logo already uses the tagline, so keep the banner simple. Make it in Canva using the free "YouTube Banner" template (2560 × 1440; keep text inside the centre 1546 × 423 safe area so it shows on phones):

> **WORK REWIRED**
> The money & tech behind how we work · New breakdown every week

Use the same dark navy as your logo, with one accent colour (e.g. bright cyan or yellow) for thumbnails too.

## 4. Channel keywords
Studio → **Settings → Channel → Basic info → Keywords**:

```
future of work, business explained, how companies make money, economics explained, tech business, AI and jobs, gig economy, business breakdown, unit economics, startup collapse, Work Rewired
```

## 5. Upload defaults (saves time on every video)
Studio → **Settings → Upload defaults**:
- **Category:** Education
- **Video language** and **title & description language:** English
- **Description template** (pre-fills every upload):
  ```
  [2–3 sentence summary]

  Chapters
  0:00

  Sources
  [paste from Claude's output]

  Work Rewired — the future of work, decoded. Subscribe for a new breakdown every week.
  ```
- **Comments:** On, with "Hold potentially inappropriate comments for review."

## 6. On each upload
- Upload the `.srt` file from `voiceover.py` under **Subtitles**. Accurate captions help viewers and search.
- **Altered or synthetic content:** answer honestly. Tick "Yes" if the video shows anything realistic that's AI-generated (realistic AI images or footage of real-looking people or events). Narration with your consistent AI voice over charts and screenshots isn't impersonating anyone, but if you're unsure, ticking "Yes" is the safe choice.
- Add the video to a **playlist** by format (e.g. "One Ride, One Order: Unit Economics", "Company Collapses", "The Economics of Owning…"). Playlists lengthen viewing sessions.
- Add an **end screen** pointing to your previous video.
